# ConCat attendee-side feature inventory

- **Source:** a clean extract of ConCat `master`, commit f4d531f (2026-11-04), at a clean `git archive` extract of ConCat master (paths are relative to the ConCat repo root). All paths below are relative to that folder. Nothing outside this extract was used.
- **Scope:** everything an attendee, parent or guardian, dealer applicant, volunteer applicant, or anonymous visitor can do.

## Conventions used in this file

- **Labels.** Quoted labels are the resolved `en-US` strings from `src/shared/translations/en-US.json`, a flat map whose keys contain dots.
  - `%%0` and `%%1` are runtime placeholders. They are shown here as `{event}`, `{n}` and similar.
  - A convention can override any UI string through `convention.i18nOverrides`. These are applied before the language dictionary, in every language, so live labels can differ from this file. See PLAT-01.
- **"Sidebar"** means the homepage side navigation (`Sidenav` in `src/frontend/containers/corecontainer.tsx`).
  - It renders only for a logged-in user (`ConCatApp.tsx`: `shouldShowSidenav = loaded && … && !!user`).
  - On mobile it opens with the header button "Open Menu".
- **Section headers in the sidebar** appear in this order:
  1. The convention long name.
  2. **Event store**.
  3. **Applications**.
  4. **Account**.
  5. **Theme Settings**.
- **"Logged in"** means the route is wrapped in `UserRoute`. A logged-out visitor is sent to `/login?redirect=<path>` and comes back after logging in.
- **Inferences.** Anything not read directly from code is marked *(inference)*.
- **Hotel and email sections.** These came from two focused sub-investigations. I spot-checked their key claims against the source: `hotelsEnabled` has no admin toggle, `hotelGroupOpen` has no sender, `paymentSuccess` needs an item with price > 0, and mail for sub-accounts falls back to the owner.

## (c) Sub-accounts and minor/dependent registration (for the Quickstart)

**What a sub-account is.** A sub-account is a person (user record) owned by another account (`user.ownerId` = the parent's ID). The parent registers, edits and pays for them from the parent's own login. All their registrations go into the parent's cart and order.

Sub-accounts have no username or password of their own, and named ones are created without an email. Mail meant for them (e.g. the registration QR) goes to the parent's address.

There are two kinds:
- **Named attendee** (adult or teen): first and last name, DOB, address, phone.
- **PII-free child:** no name, email, username or DOB. Shown as "Child#{id}" with a "Child" badge, and treated as one year under the child age threshold.

**Organizer settings.** Housekeeping › Registration settings › **"Sub-accounts"** ("Let one account register additional attendees, including children with no personal details."):
- **"Max Sub-Accounts Registration"** (`maxSubAccounts`): the number of *additional* attendees. 0 turns the feature off. The dashboard shows "{n} / {max+1}".
- **"Allow PII-free child registration"**: adds "Register child (under {n})".
- **"Only allow child sub-accounts"** (needs the previous setting): every additional attendee must be a PII-free child. The account holder still registers themselves.
- Age thresholds live in "Minimum Age and Children": "Child Age Threshold" and "Minor Age Threshold".

**Parent workflow (exact labels):**
1. Log in. Go to Sidebar › "Attendance" (dashboard) and register yourself first via "Start registration": "Registration Type" → "Select" → "Additional Information" → "Register".
   - Until you do, the dashboard says "Enter your own registration details first before adding other attendees."
2. Under **"Your registrations"** the **"Attendees"** box shows "{n} / {max}" and "You can register {n} more attendees under your account." Pick one of these:
   - **"Register another attendee"**, or "Register another attendee (Ages {n}+)" when child mode is on. The wizard starts at **"New user"**: "First Name", "Last Name", optional "Preferred Name", "Date of birth", address (prefilled from yours) and "Phone number". Then "Continue" → "Registration Type" → "Additional Information" → "Review".
     - If the date of birth is under the child threshold and PII-free children are allowed, the wizard says "Attendees under {n} cannot be registered with a name. Register them as a child instead." with the link "Register child (under {n})".
     - Otherwise, you must tick the organizer's child-registration terms checkbox.
   - **"Register child (under {n})"**. No personal details step. Tick the organizer's child-registration terms checkbox, which enables the tier buttons. Then "Select" a tier → fill the form (emergency contact "Contact Name" / "Contact Phone" is required) → "Register".
3. Each person gets a card numbered 1, 2, …, with "Status" set to "Not registered", "Unpaid", "Registered" or "Refund requested".
   - The dashboard status card turns into "Complete your registration" / "These attendees are not yet paid for:", followed by the list.
   - Press **"Complete registration"** (`/pay`) to pay for everyone in one checkout.
   - After payment the card says "All set! You and {n} others are ready to attend {event}:".
4. Card **▾** menu for a sub-account:
   - "Edit personal details" (modal; not for children)
   - "View order (#…)"
   - "Delete registration" (unpaid)
   - "Transfer registration" (if enabled)
   - "Migrate Sub-Account"
   - "Delete attendee" (unpaid only; confirm "Are you sure you want to delete this attendee? This action cannot be undone.")
5. **Hand over the account when the dependent is old enough:** "Migrate Sub-Account".
   - Enter **your** password ("Enter **Your** Password") and **their** email ("{name}'s Email Address"), then "Migrate". This shows "Account migration started."
   - They receive "Account Migration Confirmation". The link opens "Account Migration" / "Almost Done!", where they set "Username" and "Password", then "Submit". This shows "Account successfully migrated."
   - Not possible for PII-free children, for anyone under 13, or for anyone under the child threshold.

**Limits and errors:**
- "You have reached the maximum number of attendees under one account." (cap)
- "This event does not allow that kind of sub-account." (wrong kind for the settings)
- The cap counts sub-accounts that hold a registration *and* the number of sub-accounts owned.

**Not to confuse with:**
- The legacy **"Child registration"** page (`/convention/children`, badge-name-only child badges attached to the parent's registration). It is deprecated and hidden unless `maxChildren > 0`.
- The **minor sign-up** (SUB-08), where a teen in the child-to-minor age band creates their *own* account and sees the organizer's parental-consent text (`underageWarning`).
- **Dealer assistants**, who can be picked instantly from "Your Sub-accounts" (DEAL-05).
