## How to read these guides

Guides in this format share the conventions below. [Product Options](/docs/guides/products/options) is a complete example.

### Steps and results

A task is a numbered list of steps. A **Result** line under a step says what you should see once the step has worked, so you can check each one before moving on. Try it here:

1. Click the light and dark mode button in the top bar of this site. Its tooltip reads *Switch between dark and light mode*.

   <Result>The site switches between its light and dark colours. Click the button again to switch back.</Result>

### On-screen labels

<Sidenote title="Why exact labels">
Matching labels word for word makes each step easy to find on screen and easy to test.
</Sidenote>

Bold text is an exact label from the ConCat app: a menu item, button, field or message. A path such as **Housekeeping › Registration › Options** means clicking each item in turn.

### Callouts

Coloured boxes hold information that sits outside the steps. Each kind has its own label and colour, listed in Table 2.1.

<TableFigure title="Callouts used in these guides">

| Label | Colour | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In this guide | Blue | What the guide covers, at the top of the page. |
| Key idea | Blue | The one idea to take away from a section. |
| Watch out | Amber | Something that can go wrong or can't be undone. |
| Tip | Green | A quicker or better way to do something. |
| Note | Grey | Extra detail you can skip. |
| Danger | Red | A step that can lose data or money. |

</TableFigure>

:::info[Key idea]
If what you see doesn't match a step's result, stop there. The steps after it assume that one worked.
:::

### Margin notes, figures and tables

<Sidenote title="Margin notes">
Notes like this one sit in the margin on wide screens and between paragraphs on narrow ones. You can skip them.
</Sidenote>

Screenshots are numbered figures. Red numbered markers on a screenshot match the numbered notes in its caption, which sits beside the image on wide screens. Tables are numbered the same way, by section: Table 2.1 is the first table in section 2.
