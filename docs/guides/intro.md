# Introduction

Welcome to ConCat! These guides take you through setting up and running your event, one task at a time.

:::info[In this guide]
How to read the guides.
:::

## How to read these guides

These guides share the conventions below.

### Steps and results

A task is a numbered list of steps. The **Result** under each step says what you should see once it has worked, so you can check it before going on. Try it:

1. Click the light and dark mode button on the right side of the top bar.

   <Result>The site switches between light and dark colors. Click the button again to switch back.</Result>

### On-screen labels

Bold text is an exact label from ConCat: a menu item, button, field or message. A path such as **Housekeeping › Registration › Options** means clicking each item in turn.

### Callouts

Colored boxes hold information outside the steps. Table 1.1 lists each kind.

<TableFigure title="Callouts used in these guides">

| Label                                            | Color | What it holds                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ----- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <Admonition type="info" title="In this guide" /> | Blue  | What the guide covers.                                            |
| <Admonition type="info" title="Key idea" />      | Blue  | The one idea to take from a section.                              |
| <Admonition type="caution" title="Watch out" />  | Amber | Something that can go wrong or can't be undone.                   |
| <Admonition type="tip" title="Tip" />            | Green | A quicker or better way.                                          |
| <Admonition type="note" title="Note" />          | Gray  | Extra detail you can skip.                                        |
| <Admonition type="danger" title="Danger" />      | Red   | Take extra caution and fully understand before making this action |

</TableFigure>

:::info[Key idea]
If what you see doesn't match a step's result, stop there: the steps after it assume it worked.
:::

### Example values

Where your own convention's names, prices and dates will differ, the guides show example values, such as <Sample>Star26</Sample> for a convention's short name. ConCat shows your own.

An example value has a light blue tint, a dotted underline and a small "e.g.", even inside an exact label such as **Editing Option: <Sample>Pronouns</Sample>**. Hover over one, Tab to it or tap it to see _Example value_.

### Margin notes, figures and tables

<Sidenote title="Margin notes">
Notes like this sit in the margin on wide screens and between paragraphs on narrow ones. You can skip them.
</Sidenote>

Screenshots are numbered figures. Red numbered markers on a screenshot match the numbered notes in its caption. Tables are numbered by section: Table 1.1 is the first table in section 1.

:::note[Found a mistake?]
If a step doesn't match what you see, contact ConCat Support and tell them the guide, section and step.
:::
