# Start here

Get a trial instance and learn your way around Housekeeping, where all setup happens. The next four guides then set up your convention, registration, store and staff.

:::info[In this guide]
Starting a trial and signing in (section 1), and finding your way around Housekeeping (section 2).
:::

## Start a trial

<Sidenote title="What you get">
A private ConCat instance with its own web address and your administrator account, for 30 days. No card is needed.
</Sidenote>

1. Go to the [trial sign-up page](/trial) and fill in **First name**, **Last name**, **Email address**, **Business or entity name** and **Country of registration**.

   <Result>Every field is filled in. Use an email address you can open now: it becomes your sign-in.</Result>

2. Tick the reCAPTCHA box and click **Create my trial instance**.

   <Result>The form is replaced by **Check your inbox**, and an email with the subject *Confirm your email to start your ConCat trial* arrives.</Result>

3. In that email, click **Confirm email & set password**.

   <Result>The **Set your password** page opens and says **Email confirmed**.</Result>

4. Enter a password in **Password** and **Confirm password**, tick the reCAPTCHA box, and click **Set password & create my instance**.

   <Result>The **Creating your instance** page lists six steps, from **Email confirmed** to **Sign you in**, and shows **Your address**.</Result>

5. Wait for the steps to finish, usually about a minute.

   <Result>The page says **Your instance is ready**, then opens your Housekeeping **Dashboard**. An email with the subject *Your ConCat trial instance is ready* arrives, linking to your instance, its admin dashboard and its sign-in page.</Result>

:::note[If something goes wrong]
- *This link is no longer valid*: the confirmation link was already used or is over 7 days old. Start again from step 1.
- *We hit a snag*: your details and password are saved and ConCat has been told; you'll get an email when the instance is ready. Quote the **Reference** shown if you contact support.
- *You already have a ConCat trial*: this email replaces the confirmation if your address has already started a trial.
:::

Until the trial ends, every page shows a banner: **Trial instance.** *This trial ends on* and the date, 30 days after you signed up.

## Find your way around Housekeeping

<Sidenote title="Two sides of one site">
Attendees use your instance's homepage; staff use Housekeeping, its administration side. Only accounts with administrative access see the **Housekeeping** link.
</Sidenote>

Housekeeping is split into modules, each with its own sidebar. A menu at the top of the sidebar switches between them.

1. On your instance's homepage, click **Housekeeping** in the sidebar, under your convention's name.

   <Result>The **Dashboard** opens. The button at the top of the sidebar shows **Attendees**, the module you're in.</Result>

2. Click the **Attendees** button at the top of the sidebar (Figure 2.1).

   <Result>A menu lists the modules your account can use (Table 2.1).</Result>

3. Choose **Registration**.

   <Result>The button now shows **Registration**, and the sidebar shows the Registration menu, with **Badges and Tiers** further down.</Result>

4. Click **Back to Homepage** at the top of the sidebar.

   <Result>Your homepage opens, as attendees see it.</Result>

<TableFigure title="The Housekeeping modules and their menus">

| Module | Sidebar items |
| --- | --- |
| Attendees | **Dashboard**, **Cashier UI**, **Devices** <small>(hidden when QuikTicket is off)</small>; under *Module Management*, when hotels are on: **Hotel Management**; under *Attendee Management*: **User Search**, **User Notes**, **Fee Management**, **Refund Requests**, **Mass Email**, **Event Statistics** |
| Registration | **Dashboard**, **Registration Statistics**, **Mass Email**, **Settings**, **Dynamic Export**; under *Badges and Tiers*: **Badge Products**, **Options**, **Badge Art**, **Badge Designer**, **Registration Flags** |
| Store Management | **Store Products**, **Options**, **Surcharges**, **Vouchers**, **Orders**, **Gatekeeping** |
| Volunteers | **Dashboard**, **Applications**, **My Department(s)** <small>(department leads only)</small>, **Mass Email**, **Volunteer Stats**; under *Settings*: **Options**, **Volunteer Settings** |
| Dealers | **Dashboard**, **Applications**, **Areas**, **Products**, **Mass Email**, **Options**, **Dynamic Export**; under *Settings*: **Dealer Settings** |
| System | **Dynamic Export**, **Export**, **User Creation**, **Audit Logs**, **Developers**, **Settings**, **Roles** |

</TableFigure>

Each item appears only if your account has its permission.

:::info[Key idea]
Menu paths start with **Housekeeping**, then the module, then the sidebar item: **Housekeeping › System › Settings** means click **Housekeeping**, choose **System** from the module menu, then click **Settings** in the sidebar.
:::
