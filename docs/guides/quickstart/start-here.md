# Start here

This Quickstart takes a new event from a trial sign-up to ready for attendees. This first guide gets you a trial instance and shows you around Housekeeping, where all the setup happens. The four guides after it set up your convention, registration, store and staff, in that order.

:::info[In this guide]
How to start a trial and sign in for the first time (section 1), and how to find your way around Housekeeping (section 2).
:::

## Start a trial

<Sidenote title="What you get">
A trial is a private ConCat instance with its own web address and your administrator account. It lasts 30 days, and no card is needed.
</Sidenote>

1. Go to the [trial sign-up page](/trial) and fill in **First name**, **Last name**, **Email address**, **Business or entity name** and **Country of registration**.

   <Result>Every field is filled in. The email address becomes your sign-in, so use one you can open now.</Result>

2. Tick the reCAPTCHA box, then click **Create my trial instance**.

   <Result>The form is replaced by **Check your inbox**, and an email with the subject *Confirm your email to start your ConCat trial* arrives.</Result>

3. In that email, click **Confirm email & set password**.

   <Result>A page titled **Set your password** opens. It says **Email confirmed** and shows your **Email**, with empty **Password** and **Confirm password** fields.</Result>

4. Enter a password in **Password** and again in **Confirm password**, tick the reCAPTCHA box, then click **Set password & create my instance**.

   <Result>The **Creating your instance** page lists six steps: **Email confirmed**, **Password set**, **Reserve address**, **Create instance**, **Apply trial settings** and **Sign you in**. It also shows **Your address**.</Result>

5. Wait for the steps to finish. This usually takes about a minute.

   <Result>The page says **Your instance is ready** and **Signing you in…**, then opens your instance's Housekeeping **Dashboard**. An email with the subject *Your ConCat trial instance is ready* arrives, with links to your instance, its admin dashboard and its sign-in page.</Result>

:::note[If something goes wrong]
- *This link is no longer valid*: the confirmation link has already been used, or is more than 7 days old. Start again from step 1.
- *We hit a snag*: your details and password are saved, and ConCat has been told. You'll get an email when the instance is ready. Quote the **Reference** shown on the page if you contact support.
- *You already have a ConCat trial*: this email arrives instead of the confirmation if your email address has already started a trial.
:::

Until the trial ends, every page of your instance shows a banner: **Trial instance.** *This trial ends on* the date 30 days after you signed up.

## Find your way around Housekeeping

<Sidenote title="Two sides of one site">
Attendees use the homepage of your instance. Staff use Housekeeping, the administration side of the same site. Only accounts with administrative access see the **Housekeeping** link.
</Sidenote>

Housekeeping is split into modules: Attendees, Registration, Store Management, Volunteers, Dealers and System. A menu at the top of the sidebar switches between them, and each module has its own sidebar.

1. On your instance's homepage, click **Housekeeping** in the sidebar, just under your convention's name.

   <Result>The **Dashboard** opens, with **Total Registrations** and **Total Paid Registrations** cards. At the top of the sidebar is a button showing **Attendees**, the module you're in.</Result>

2. Click the **Attendees** button at the top of the sidebar.

   <Result>A menu lists the modules your account can use: **Attendees**, **Registration**, **Store Management**, **Volunteers**, **Dealers** and **System**.</Result>

3. Choose **Registration**.

   <Result>The sidebar changes to the Registration menu, headed **Registration**, with **Badges and Tiers** further down. The button at the top now shows **Registration**.</Result>

4. Click **Back to Homepage** at the top of the sidebar.

   <Result>Your instance's homepage opens, as attendees see it.</Result>

<TableFigure title="The Housekeeping modules and their menus">

| Module | Sidebar items |
| --- | --- |
| Attendees | **Dashboard**, **Cashier UI**, **Devices** <small>(hidden when QuikTicket is turned off)</small>; under *Module Management*, when hotels are on: **Hotel Management**; under *Attendee Management*: **User Search**, **User Notes**, **Fee Management**, **Refund Requests**, **Mass Email**, **Event Statistics** |
| Registration | **Dashboard**, **Registration Statistics**, **Mass Email**, **Settings**, **Dynamic Export**; under *Badges and Tiers*: **Badge Products**, **Options**, **Badge Art**, **Badge Designer**, **Registration Flags** |
| Store Management | **Store Products**, **Options**, **Surcharges**, **Vouchers**, **Orders**, **Gatekeeping** |
| Volunteers | **Dashboard**, **Applications**, **My Department(s)** <small>(department leads only)</small>, **Mass Email**, **Volunteer Stats**; under *Settings*: **Options**, **Volunteer Settings** |
| Dealers | **Dashboard**, **Applications**, **Areas**, **Products**, **Mass Email**, **Options**, **Dynamic Export**; under *Settings*: **Dealer Settings** |
| System | **Dynamic Export**, **Export**, **User Creation**, **Audit Logs**, **Developers**, **Settings**, **Roles** |

</TableFigure>

Each item appears only if your account has the permission for it, so a staff member may see fewer.

:::info[Key idea]
Menu paths in these guides start with **Housekeeping**, then the module, then the sidebar item. **Housekeeping › System › Settings** means: click **Housekeeping**, choose **System** from the module menu, then click **Settings** in the sidebar.
:::
