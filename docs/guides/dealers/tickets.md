# Tickets

The Dealers module has no tickets of its own. Timed entry tickets with QR codes, such as entry to a dealers' den at a set time, are made with ticketed products, which work the same way for any part of your event. The [Ticketed products](../products/tickets.md) in the Products section explains how they work and what attendees see.
