# Languages and wording

ConCat's screens come in four languages, and each person picks their own. See how to switch, what isn't translated, and how to change ConCat's wording.

:::info[In this guide]
The four languages, choosing a language, what isn't translated, changing ConCat's wording.
:::

## The four languages

<Sidenote title="Labels in these guides">
These guides quote the **English (US)** wording. Other languages translate the same words.
</Sidenote>

Table 1.1 lists the languages as they appear in the language list.

<TableFigure title="Interface languages">

| Language | Code |
| --- | --- |
| **English (US)** | `en-US` |
| **Português (Brasil)** | `pt-BR` |
| **English (UK)** | `en-GB` |
| **日本語 (Japan)** | `ja-JP` |

</TableFigure>

Untranslated text shows in English (US). **English (UK)** differs from English (US) in only a few words.

On a first visit, ConCat uses the browser's language if it's one of the four, otherwise English (US).

## Choose a language

Each person chooses in their own browser, and the choice applies to every page, Housekeeping included.

1. Scroll to the bottom of any page.

   <Result>The footer shows a language list, with the **Terms & Conditions** and **Privacy Policy** links.</Result>

2. Open the language list and choose a language.

   <Result>The page's menus, buttons and messages change to that language.</Result>

3. Reload the page.

   <Result>The page stays in that language: this browser remembers the choice.</Result>

:::note[One browser at a time]
The choice is saved in the browser, not the account. On another device or browser, the person chooses again.
:::

## What isn't translated

ConCat translates its own screens, not the content you write:

- **Emails** are sent as you wrote the templates, in one language, to everyone. Dates in emails use the US English format.
- **Your content**, such as product names, policies, form questions and department descriptions, appears exactly as you entered it.

If your attendees speak several languages, write that content in each.

## Changing ConCat's wording

<Sidenote title="One convention at a time">
Wording changes are per convention. Your other conventions keep ConCat's own wording.
</Sidenote>

ConCat can replace any of its on-screen wording for one convention, for example to call badges "memberships". There's no Housekeeping screen for this: send ConCat Support the text to change and what it should say.

:::caution[Watch out]
A replaced wording shows in every language. Change a label to English text, and people using Português (Brasil) or 日本語 (Japan) see your English text too.
:::
