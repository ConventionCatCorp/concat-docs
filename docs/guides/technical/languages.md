# Languages and wording

ConCat's screens come in four languages, and each person picks their own. This guide lists the languages, shows how to switch, and explains what stays in the language you wrote it in.

:::info[In this guide]
The four languages (section 1), choosing a language (section 2), what isn't translated (section 3), and changing ConCat's wording for your event (section 4).
:::

## The four languages

<Sidenote title="Labels in these guides">
These guides quote the **English (US)** wording. In another language, the labels are translations of the same words.
</Sidenote>

Table 1.1 lists the languages, as they appear in the language list.

<TableFigure title="Interface languages">

| Language | Code |
| --- | --- |
| **English (US)** | `en-US` |
| **Português (Brasil)** | `pt-BR` |
| **English (UK)** | `en-GB` |
| **日本語 (Japan)** | `ja-JP` |

</TableFigure>

Not every screen is fully translated yet. Any text without a translation is shown in English (US). **English (UK)** differs from English (US) only in a few words.

When someone first visits, ConCat uses their browser's language if it is one of the four. Otherwise it shows English (US).

## Choose a language

The language is a choice each person makes for themselves, in their own browser. It applies to every page, including Housekeeping.

1. Scroll to the bottom of any page.

   <Result>The footer shows the **Powered by ConCat** logo, **Version:** and a number, a language list, and the **Terms & Conditions** and **Privacy Policy** links.</Result>

2. Open the language list and choose a language.

   <Result>The page's menus, buttons and messages change to the language you chose.</Result>

3. Reload the page.

   <Result>The page stays in the language you chose. ConCat remembers the choice in this browser.</Result>

:::note[One browser at a time]
The choice is saved in the browser, not in the account. On another device or browser, the person chooses again.
:::

## What isn't translated

ConCat translates its own screens, not the content you write.

- **Emails** are sent as you wrote the templates, in one language, to everyone. Dates in emails use the US English format.
- **Your content**, such as product names, policies, form questions and department descriptions, appears exactly as you entered it.

If your attendees speak more than one language, write that content in each language they need.

## Changing ConCat's wording

<Sidenote title="One convention at a time">
Wording changes are set per convention. Other conventions in your organization keep ConCat's own wording.
</Sidenote>

ConCat can replace any piece of its on-screen wording for one convention, for example to call badges "memberships". There is no screen in Housekeeping for this. Ask ConCat Support, and tell them the text you want to change and what it should say.

:::caution[Watch out]
A replaced wording is shown in every language. If you change a label to English text, people using Português (Brasil) or 日本語 (Japan) also see your English text.
:::
